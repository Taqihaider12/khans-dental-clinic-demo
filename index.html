The practice

A family clinic that grew into a multi-specialty floor

Dr. Mumtaz A. Khan opened the doors in 1981. Today the same address holds paediatric rooms, conscious sedation, CAD/CAM ceramics, and consultants in implants and periodontics — without sending you across the city for a second opinion.

Seven operatories

Parallel chairs so check-ups, surgery, and children’s visits do not bottleneck one corridor.

Imaging on site

Digital 3D OPG in the building. You leave with a plan, not a referral slip for another clinic.

Sedation when needed

Nitrous oxide for anxious adults and children, monitored on the chair — not a hospital admission by default.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about dental anxiety, treatments, or pediatric care? Here are our specialists' answers.

We provide specialized Conscious Sedation (commonly known as laughing gas/Nitrous Oxide) to eliminate dental anxiety safely. It induces a relaxed, floating sensation, allowing both anxious children and adults to receive procedures completely stress-free under full monitoring.
Yes, we have custom child-friendly operatories specifically decorated for pediatric patients. Led by Dr. Urooj Mumtaz, our staff uses cartoons, movies, toys, and gentle clinical communication techniques to ensure children enjoy their dental visit and avoid developing clinic phobias.
Our multi-specialty practice is equipped with premium in-house digital 3D OPG imaging systems for ultra-low radiation panoramic X-Rays. We also partner with leading foreign prosthetic labs and utilize advanced CAD/CAM ceramic milling technology for same-day crown and veneer placements.
